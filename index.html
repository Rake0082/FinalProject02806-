Age

Does age change your risk?

Fatality rate rises sharply with age — but the effect is far steeper for pedestrians than for cyclists or motorists.

Time & demographics

When are crashes most deadly?

Rush hour has the most crashes — but late-night crashes (midnight–4 am) are disproportionately fatal across all three personas.
